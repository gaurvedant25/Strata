## 7. Availability, sync, and the honest limits of both

Gym Log is offered as it is, and it is free. There is no uptime commitment and no guaranteed level of service.

The app is built to work offline, and your device holds the authoritative copy of your log. Sync is a best-effort reconciliation on top of that; it can be delayed, and it can fail, without your local log being affected.

Two limits are worth stating in plain terms rather than burying:

- **Conflicts are resolved last-write-wins.** If the same record is changed on two devices while both are offline, the edit with the later timestamp is kept and the earlier one is lost. This is the right trade for one person's training log, but it can lose an edit.
- **This relies on your devices' clocks being roughly correct.** A device with a badly wrong clock can order edits wrongly.
