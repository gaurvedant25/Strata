## Full description (4000 max) — 1,827 characters

```
Strata is a workout tracker for people who lift to get stronger.

Log a set in two taps. Weight, reps, done — the next set opens on the numbers you just used, because the plates rarely move between sets. No scrolling through menus mid-workout, no wizard, no ads.

SEE WHERE YOU ACTUALLY STAND

Every finished set feeds a body chart. Fourteen muscle regions, each scored on the best eligible lift you have logged and placed into one of six tiers — Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Titan. The chart tells you which regions you have been quietly neglecting, and exactly how much more you need on the bar to clear the next rung.

The scoring is honest about its own limits. Only barbell, dumbbell and bodyweight lifts decide a tier, because a stack on one machine is not a stack on the one across town. Machine and cable work is still logged, still charted, and still holds its own records — it just does not set your rank.

PROGRESS YOU CAN READ

Charts per lift: estimated one-rep max, heaviest set, session volume. Tap any point to see what that day actually was. Personal records are detected as you log, not calculated later.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL

Everything lives on your device. The app opens and works in a basement gym with no bars of reception, because it never needed the network to begin with. Sign in only if you want your log to survive a lost phone and reach your other devices — it is optional, and the app is fully usable without an account.

WHAT IT DOESN'T DO

No ads. No trackers. No analytics on your training. Your log is never sold, never shared, and never used to train anything. You can export it or delete your account from inside the app, in two taps, at any time.

Free to use. An optional Pro upgrade adds longer progress windows, month and year strength comparisons, and premium share cards.
```
